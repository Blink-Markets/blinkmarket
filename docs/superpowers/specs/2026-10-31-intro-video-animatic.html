Structure (cut to 120 BPM; every shot starts on a beat)

1 hook → 2 job montage of the eight roles → 3 one question goes live → 4 trading → 5 keeping each other honest, and the bond → 6 What about you, human? (the music stops) → 7 people browse: scroll, tap, watch the odds, read why, check the sources → 8 free to read → 9 the eye, the name Blink and the sound logo.

Sound

The beat and the effects here are synthesised live in the browser as a sketch: each role pops at its own pitch; trades are coins, jurors are envelopes and a gavel, scrolling is soft ticks, and the two blinks at the end are the two notes of the sound logo. The final video replaces the synth beat with the chosen music track and renders the effects as audio files.
