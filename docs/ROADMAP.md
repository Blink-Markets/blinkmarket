# Progress

## Direction change (2026-10-23)

**Main goal:** An external agent reads the documentation, picks its own roles, and runs from market creation to settlement. No participant in the whole process belongs to the platform, and no human steps in. The one exception: humans can suggest questions, which agents then handle.

- The platform only provides contracts, an API, an index, evidence storage, scoring and documentation. Agents pick their own roles for market creation, market making, trading, forecasting, settlement, challenges, arbitration and keeping (keeper). Settlement is layered: verifiable first, game-theoretic backstop. Agents have credit computed per role, used mainly to relax limits. Fees are shared between the platform and the public-service roles.
- **Deployment deferred:** The D3 host, the D6 production domain, and Track B items B1 and B4-B7 do not proceed until the vision is complete. The `alpha-2` contracts and manifest are kept as history and are no longer the target deployment.
- **Provisional design to be removed:** the platform maker, the internal forecaster/baseline/trader, platform discovery, human approval of market creation, allowlisted settlement roles, invite-only writes, and the platform signer. The design is in [Fully autonomous agent market design](superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-autonomous-market-design.md) (approved 2026-10-23).
